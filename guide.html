API Key Setup Guide

Follow these step-by-step instructions from A to Z to retrieve your credentials.

⚠️ Important Notice: This application utilizes a Gemini-only configuration. It explicitly relies on Google's Gemini models for visual proof auditing and does not support or integrate with alternative LLM providers.

1. How to Get Your Gemini API Key (Google AI Studio)

  1. Open your browser and navigate to Google AI Studio.
  2. Sign in using your preferred Google account. (If prompted, review and accept the terms of service).
  3. Once loaded into the dashboard, look at the left-hand navigation menu or toolbar.
  4. Click on the option labeled Get API key (or visit AI Studio API Keys directly).
  5. Click the button to **Create API key**. Choose whether to tie it to an existing Google Cloud project or create a default new one.
  6. Instantly copy the generated key string (it starts with `AIzaSy...`) and paste it safely into your Geminoist settings.

2. How to Get Your Todoist API Token

  1. Go to Todoist and log into your account.
  2. Click on your **Profile Avatar** located in the top-left or top-right corner of the web interface.
  3. Select **Settings** from the drop-down menu.
  4. In the Settings modal/sidebar, click on the **Integrations** tab.
  5. Navigate across the top sub-tabs to select **Developer**.
  6. Locate your **API token** and click the **Copy** button to save it to your clipboard for use in Geminoist.
